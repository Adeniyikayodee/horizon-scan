# Output spec

## Innovation map row

One row per approach, under its theme: theme, approach name, what it is, year,
overall fit, verification status, source link. Every row rests on a primary
source with a title, a link, and a date.

## Theme scorecard

One row per theme: name, standing (existing, adjacent, new), the four marks,
posture, whether it is a top area, and a marquee approach. One intro paragraph
on top.

## Memo shape

Two to three pages: the leading approaches grouped by theme with their postures,
the new and adjacent areas worth taking forward, and the open questions. Lead
with the two cleanest new areas.

## House style (ACET)

- US English, active voice, serial comma.
- Spell out zero to nine, digits for 10 and up.
- Write out million, billion, trillion. "percent" in prose, % in tables.
- US dates (June 20, 2026). Double quotes in text, single in headings.
- No em dashes, use commas or rewrite.
- Write in the analyst's own voice. Describe findings, not how they were gathered.
- No trace of tools or AI, not in cells, prose, comments, or file properties.
- Author and last-modified are Kayode Adeniyi.
- Where a claim rests on secondary sources only, say so and mark it partial. Write "not found" where you cannot confirm, never invent.
