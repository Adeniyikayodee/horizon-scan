# Scoring

Score each approach, and each theme, on four criteria, using strong, partial,
or weak. Weight the first two most, they sit closest to ACET's identity.

- Mandate fit: does it advance economic transformation as ACET defines it, GDP growth together with gains in DEPTH (Diversification, Export competitiveness, Productivity, Technology upgrading, Human well-being).
- Research to policy: could ACET turn it into policy advice that moves.
- African traction: does the continent show real demand and uptake.
- White space: does the area stand open for ACET, with room to lead. This is the primary screen.

Give a one-line reason for every mark, drawn only from the evidence in front of
you. Keep source facts separate from your own reading, and label any inference.

## Overall fit

Resolve the marks into an overall fit, high, medium, or low, leaning on mandate
fit and research to policy.

## Posture (themes only)

Each theme carries one posture:

- enter: a new or adjacent area worth entering or piloting now, where ACET would define an agenda its peers have not taken up.
- watch: monitor and revisit at the next scan.
- deepen: existing work that strongly matches ACET's current focus, to keep current. Every existing theme is deepen.

Self-check: no existing theme may carry enter.
