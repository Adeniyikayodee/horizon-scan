# Policy

The non-negotiables the guardrail enforces over every output.

## Primary-source-only

A row cannot read "verified" without a confirming quote from the institution's
own page or report. Without one it is downgraded to partial. Never invent a
source, a figure, or a program. Write "not found" where you cannot confirm.

## No tool or AI trace

Nothing in any deliverable, cell, prose line, or file property may name or hint
at a tool or model, or at how the work was gathered. Keep retrieval mechanics
out: no "blocked", "HTTP 403", "search snippet", or platform names. Write in the
analyst's voice, describe findings, not how they were found.

## Style hard rules

- No em dashes or en dashes.
- Author and last-modified are Kayode Adeniyi on every file.
